# 📊 Retail Customers and Sales Dashboard

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project analyzes : product performance like revenue generated ,quantity sold and stock for each category,brand, product; sales analytics like average and total amount of sales around the year,number of orders around the year per payment method; customer analytics like customer distribution according to gender,country.

The dashboard was built using Power BI and leverages **DAX measures** for deeper insights into Customer lifetime value(CLV), quantity of products purchased, revenue generated by the products.

You can also drill through the pages using customer gender and the country to see how different customer demographics affect different products sales.

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset includes:

### Customers
- customer_id (int): Unique identifier for each customer
- name (string): Customer full name
- email (string): Customer email address
- gender (string): Customer gender ('Male', 'Female', 'Other')
- signup_date (date): Date customer signed up
- country (string): Customer country of residence

### Products
- product_id (int): Unique identifier for each product
- product_name (string): Name of the product
- category (string): Product category (e.g., Electronics, Books)
- price (float): Price per unit
- stock_quantity (int): Available stock count
- brand (string): Product brand name

### Orders
- order_id (int): Unique identifier for each order
- customer_id (int): ID of the customer who placed the order (foreign key to Customers)
- order_date (date): Date when order was placed
- total_amount (float): Total amount for the order
- payment_method (string): Payment method used (Credit Card, PayPal, etc.)
- shipping_country (string): Country where the order is shipped

### Order Items
- order_item_id (int): Unique identifier for each order item
- order_id (int): ID of the order this item belongs to (foreign key to Orders)
- product_id (int): ID of the product ordered (foreign key to Products)
- quantity (int): Number of units ordered
- unit_price (float): Price per unit at order time

### Product Reviews
- review_id (int): Unique identifier for each review
- product_id (int): ID of the reviewed product (foreign key to Products)
- customer_id (int): ID of the customer who wrote the review (foreign key to Customers)
- rating (int): Rating score (1 to 5)
- review_text (string): Text content of the review
- review_date (date): Date the review was written

## 🖥️ Dashboard Features

### Customer Page
- Bar graph showing number of customers from each country sorted by the number of customers belonging to the country
- Gender distribution of customers
- Customer signing up per year basis
- CLV(Customer lifetime value) of each customers

### Sales Page
- Distribution of number of customers that made a purchase based yearly categorized by payment method
- Total Sales amount yearly categorized by payment methods.
- Average Amount of Sale quaterly
- Number of order placed from each shipping country

### Product Page
- Beakdown of total revenue based on Brand, category, products.
- Beakdown of total quantity of items sold based on Brand, category, products.
- Beakdown of total stock available based on Brand, category, products.
- Reviews of each brand and category.

## 🧮 Key DAX Measures

Some of the measures created:
- Total Quantity of the items sold adding up total of all products sold.
- Total Revenue : total amount generated adding value of each product sold considering price of each unit.
- CLV(Customer Lifetime Value) : Total amount of purchase made by each customer considering all the purchases they have made starting from signup_date till date.
